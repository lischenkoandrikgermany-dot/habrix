Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Habrix is a habit tracker. We built it so that your goals and progress stay with you. Habrix does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your tasks — habits, one-off tasks, bills, subscriptions and events you add, with their schedules and reminders.
  • Your progress — completions, XP, level, rank, series and records.
  • Your photo — the profile photo you choose, used for your avatar and share cards.
  • Settings — language, theme, end of day and reminder times.

This data is stored only in the app on your device and in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which are controlled by you and protected by Apple. It is never uploaded to our servers — we don't have any. Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone.

Photos

When you set a profile photo, iOS lets you pick a single image. Habrix receives only that image. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library and does not use the camera or microphone.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. You can turn them off in Profile → Reminders or in the Settings app.

Widgets and sharing

Widgets read your progress from the app on the same iPhone. When you share a progress card, iOS sends the image only where you choose.

No ads, no tracking

Habrix contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Habrix does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Visit Support.
