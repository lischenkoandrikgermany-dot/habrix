Support

We're happy to help. Answers to common questions are below.

Still need help? Email us at lischenko.andrik@gmail.com — we usually reply within a day.

How do I earn XP?

Mark a task as done. Easy tasks earn 10 XP, medium 20 XP and hard 40 XP. You choose the difficulty when you create the task.

How do levels and ranks work?

Going from level N to level N+1 takes 100 × N XP. Your rank follows your level: E from level 1, D from 5, C from 9, B from 14, A from 20, S from 27, SS from 35 and SSS from 44.

What is “Carried over”?

Tasks you didn't finish stay with you for up to 14 days in the “Carried over” section on Today. Tap “Complete” to earn the XP, or “Decline” to drop the task.

Why did my rank go down?

A missed task never costs XP. Only “Decline” takes the task's XP back, and if your XP falls below the rank threshold, the rank goes down too.

My series broke after midnight. Why?

Open Profile → End of day. Tasks completed at night before that hour count toward the previous day. The default is 3 a.m.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, find Habrix and choose the small, medium or large size.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open Profile → Language or Theme. Habrix is available in English, Ukrainian and Russian. “Auto” follows your iPhone.

How do I turn reminders off?

Open Profile → Reminders. You can turn off the morning, midday or evening reminder, or all of them.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Your progress is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Set up your new iPhone from that backup and everything will be there.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your iPhone. Delete tasks inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
